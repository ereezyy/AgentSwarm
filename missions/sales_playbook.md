# Sales & Outreach Strategy: From Code to Cash

## Phase 1: The "Brutally Honest" Portfolio
Before reaching out, you need proof. Build three "Showcase Agents" that solve real problems:
1.  **A Lead Scraper:** Scrapes LinkedIn/Twitter for specific keywords and writes a personalized intro.
2.  **A Support Agent:** A bot trained on a complex technical manual that answers questions perfectly.
3.  **A Content Agent:** Takes a YouTube URL and generates a blog post, 5 tweets, and a LinkedIn update.

## Phase 2: Platform Strategy (Fiverr/Upwork)
*   **Fiverr:** Focus on "Gig SEO." Use keywords like "AI Automation," "Custom AI Bot," and "Agentic Workflow."
*   **Upwork:** Don't just apply to jobs. Use the "Project Catalog" to list your pre-built packages.
*   **The Hook:** Offer a "Free 15-minute Automation Audit" in your description to get them on a call.

## Phase 3: Direct Outreach (The "Fixer" Approach)
Target niche agencies (Marketing, Real Estate, Recruiting) that are still doing manual work.
*   **The Script:** "I saw your team is doing [Manual Task]. I built an AI agent that does that in 3 seconds for $0.05. Want to see the demo?"
*   **The Close:** Don't sell "AI." Sell "Time." Instead of "I build bots," say "I give your team 20 hours back every week."

## Sales Pipeline Management

| Stage | Action | Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prospecting** | Find 10 companies/day on LinkedIn/Twitter. | Identify pain points. |
| **Initial Contact** | Send the "Fixer" script. | Get a demo call. |
| **Demo Call** | Show the showcase agents. | Confirm the value prop. |
| **Proposal** | Send a tiered pricing doc. | Close the deal. |
| **Onboarding** | Set up the agent & Slack channel. | Start the project. |
